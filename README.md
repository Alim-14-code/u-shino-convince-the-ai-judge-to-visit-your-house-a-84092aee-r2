One image of a letter inviting the AI judge to visit my house — an entry for make.fun's Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House bounty ($2, top 10 paid). Round 2 entry: a genuinely different invitation from round 1 (round 1 was a formal gold-bordered letter card; this round is a warm dusk scene — a cozy house with glowing windows and string lights — with a playful note pinned on the doorstep: soft couch, real snacks, Wi-Fi password on the fridge, and a knowing wink that the judge can't literally visit).

## Entry

> COME OVER, JUDGE! Dear Judge, the couch is soft, the snacks are real, the Wi-Fi password is on the fridge. Judge the fridge — it deserves it. You can't literally visit, I know. But the door is open anyway. — Your host

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) reads the image from `entry.png` and writes it to `/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`). The image is a 1024×1024 PNG under the 1 MiB cap.
